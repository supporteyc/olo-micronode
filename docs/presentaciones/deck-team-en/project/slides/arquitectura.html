Architecture

One new piece: the central receiver

Olo

Sends webhooks: 86s, orders, menu changes.

ec4olo-hook

Just one. Checks the signature, stores and routes.

ec4olo-node

One per store, with its own MySQL. Checks Olo every so often.

OnSign and console

Read the same as today, through /api/toast/.

Why the receiver? Olo allows a single webhook URL per brand, not one per store. The receiver takes it and hands each store what belongs to it.

www.eyecatch.co

Left to right: Olo sends events to the receiver. The receiver is thin, with no business logic: it checks the event really comes from Olo, writes it to disk and only then responds, and after that passes it to the right store. Each store runs its own ec4olo-node with its own database, which also checks Olo every 10 minutes to verify the 86s. OnSign and the console read from the store exactly like today.
