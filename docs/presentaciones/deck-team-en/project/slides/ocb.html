One detail to watch: “Ready” in Olo doesn’t mean the kitchen is done. Olo sends OrderClosed when the estimated time is reached; it doesn’t document any other signal. If a store doesn’t use Expo, OrderPickedUp never arrives, so there’s a setting to hide the order a few minutes after OrderClosed. Scheduled orders wait and show up when they fire. And when a guest modifies an order, Olo doesn’t cancel the old one: it sends a new one that replaces it, and we take the original off.
